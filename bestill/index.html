Be Still — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 4, 2026
Contact: contact@nemofactory.co.kr

In one line. Be Still has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no server that stores your information. Everything you do in the app — the days you finished, the apps you chose to lock and your settings — stays on your device. The only internet requests the app makes are checking an app status file and App Store payments (section 7).

1. Personal information we collect

None. We do not collect your name, email, contact details, date of birth, location, payment details or any other personal information. There is no account feature, so there is no sign-up or sign-in.

2. What is stored on your device only

The values below are saved in the app's private storage on your device. The values that the Screen Time extensions need are also written to a shared container used only by this app (App Group), which is also on your device and cannot be read by other apps. None of them is sent to us.

  • Journey — the dates on which you finished the day's verse, used to show your place in the journey and your streak.
  • App lock — whether the lock is on, the apps you chose (as opaque tokens from Apple; see section 3), how many minutes apps stay open after the verse, and when the current open window ends.
  • Settings — your chosen Bible text (BSB or KJV), whether the daily reminder is on and its time, and whether you have finished the first-run screens.
  • Purchase status — whether your subscription is active, and the time of the free-trial reminder (section 5). No card numbers or other payment details.

Deleting the app deletes all of these values.

3. Screen Time — how app locking works

Be Still uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls · Managed Settings · Device Activity), in which Apple deliberately hides app information from us.

  • The screen for choosing apps is a system screen drawn by Apple, and what you choose comes back as opaque tokens. We cannot read which apps you picked.
  • Applying and lifting the lock, the screen shown when you open a locked app, and locking again when the open window ends are handled on your device by Apple's system and this app's extensions.
  • Screen Time is requested only to manage your own device, never to supervise another person's device.
  • As Apple requires, Screen Time data is never sent off the device, never shared with or sold to third parties, and never used for ads or analytics.

4. Permissions — what and why

  • Screen Time (Family Controls) — the only way Apple provides for locking apps. Without it, the lock does not work; the engravings and verses still do.
  • Notifications — for the daily reminder you can turn on, the reminder one day before a free trial ends, and the notice that takes you from the lock screen to today's verse. All notifications are scheduled on your device; there is no push server.

The app does not access the camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts, calendars or health data. It does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA) and never shows the tracking (ATT) prompt.

5. Payments

Be Still Premium (the app lock) is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly, with a 3-day free trial. Payment, renewal, cancellation and refunds are handled entirely by Apple's App Store; we never see your payment details. You can cancel any time in your device's Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions. Reading the engravings and verses is free.

6. Ads and analytics

There are no ads, and no analytics or crash-reporting tools are included. We do not collect how you use the app.

7. Every request the app sends out

  • App status file — when the app starts, it reads one small file on our website (hosted on Netlify) to check whether your version must be updated. The request only downloads the file and carries no values about you. As with any web page, the hosting server's logs may record the connecting IP address. Without a connection the check is skipped.
  • App Store payments — prices, purchases and restoring purchases talk to the App Store on your device. Our server is not involved.
  • Links you tap yourself — "Privacy Policy" (this page, GitHub Pages), "Terms of Use" (Apple's standard terms page) and "Contact" (your mail app) open on your device; from there that service's own policy applies.

The engravings and Bible text are inside the app, so the journey works without the internet.

8. Content

The engravings are by Gustave Doré and the Scripture is from the Berean Standard Bible and the King James Version — all in the public domain. The app adds no commentary of its own.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
