Shortcut Hub Privacy Policy

Effective: November 5, 2026
Contact: contact@nemofactory.co.kr

In one line
Shortcut Hub has no sign-up and no login, and does not ask for personal information such as your name or contact details. There are no ads and no analytics. The app asks for no permissions. Shortcuts are run by Apple's Shortcuts app on your device, and what they do never reaches us.

1. Personal information we collect

We collect none. No personal information such as your name, contact details, email, photos or location ever reaches us. There are no accounts, so there is no sign-up or login.

Tapping Contact at the bottom of the Settings screen shows our email address. Tapping the address opens the mail app on your device, and the email you write yourself comes to us. Your mail app sends it; the app itself collects nothing. The subject line is pre-filled with the app name and version.

2. When you add a shortcut

Every shortcut file is built into the app. When you tap "Add", the app copies that file once into your device's temporary storage and hands it to the Shortcuts app through the iOS Share sheet; it does not connect to the internet to do this. Once a shortcut is in the Shortcuts app it is yours, and Shortcut Hub cannot read, change or run it.

3. When a shortcut runs

  • Apple's Shortcuts app runs it. If a shortcut wants to use information such as contacts, photos, calendars, location or health data, iOS asks you under the Shortcuts app's permissions. That information never comes to Shortcut Hub or to us.
  • AI shortcuts use Apple's Apple Intelligence model. Apple processes the request on your device or on Apple's Private Cloud Compute, under Apple's own policy. None of our shortcuts send your text to our servers or to any other company's AI.
  • Shortcuts that use Apple features such as weather, maps, translation or phone calls are handled by those Apple services. None of the shortcuts we made send requests to our servers or to third-party web addresses.
  • Automations (charger connected, battery level, alarm, arriving at or leaving a place, NFC tags and so on) are turned on by you in the Shortcuts app; the app's guides only show the steps in text.

4. Permissions

Shortcut Hub requests no permissions. It has no access to location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data or notifications, and the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt never appears.

5. Payments

Payments are handled by the App Store. "Unlock Forever" is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, so it never renews. Payment details such as card numbers go straight to Apple; we never see, receive or keep them. The app only asks the App Store on your device whether you have purchased, so it can unlock what you bought and restore purchases. Terms of use follow Apple's standard License Agreement (EULA).

6. Every request the app sends out

  • Version check: when the app opens, it reads one small file that says whether the current version can still be used. It is read-only and carries none of your data. The file is hosted on Netlify, whose server logs may record your IP address.
  • Store payments: the app talks to the App Store on your device to show prices, make purchases and restore them.
  • Opening links: tapping this policy or the terms of use opens the page in your device's browser.

The app sends no other requests to the internet. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools.

7. What is stored on your device only

Two things: whether you have bought Unlock Forever, and the language you chose in the app. The file copied to hand a shortcut over stays briefly in your device's temporary storage until iOS clears it. Everything stays on the device and is deleted when you delete the app. None of it identifies you.

8. Sharing, international transfer, sale

We pass on or sell no information to third parties. Payments are processed directly by Apple under its own policy. Apart from your IP address possibly being recorded in the server logs of Netlify (United States), which hosts the version-check file, no information is transferred abroad.

9. Retention and deletion

We keep no user data, so there is nothing for you to ask us to delete. Values on your device are deleted when you delete the app. Emails received through Contact are kept only as long as needed to reply, then deleted.

10. Children under 14

We do not knowingly collect children's personal information. The app has no ads and no tracking.

11. Your rights

Under applicable privacy laws you may request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of your personal information. However, we hold no personal information, so there is nothing to request; you can delete the values on your device yourself at any time by deleting the app.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date on this page. The revised policy takes effect on the day it is posted on this page.

Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you use the app in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, this section applies in addition to the above.

  • Controller — the controller for the little information we process ourselves is Nemo Factory (Republic of Korea). Contact: contact@nemofactory.co.kr. The Republic of Korea has adequacy decisions from the European Commission and the United Kingdom.
  • What we process and why — we do not process information stored only on your device. If you email us, we use it only to reply (legitimate interest, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) and keep it only for the period stated above. Access logs (IP address) on the server hosting the version-check file rely on our legitimate interest in running the app safely (Art. 6(1)(f)). Payments are processed by Apple as a separate controller; the app checks with the store whether you have purchased so it can unlock what you bought (performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • No ads, no tracking — we do no advertising, analytics, profiling or cross-device tracking. The values stored on your device are only those strictly necessary for features you ask for (unlocking your purchase, your chosen language).
  • Your rights — you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interest. Email us and we will reply within one month. Because we hold almost nothing that identifies you, we may be unable to find information linked to you.
  • No automated decisions — we make no decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects.
  • Children — we do not knowingly collect children's information. If we learn that we hold any, we delete it.
  • International transfers — Apple and Netlify may transfer information outside your country, including to the United States, under their own safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Complaints — you may lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in the country where you live or work.
