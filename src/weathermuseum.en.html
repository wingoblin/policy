Arto Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026
Contact: contact@nemofactory.co.kr

In short
Arto (formerly Weather Museum) has no sign-up or log-in, and we don't collect personal information such as your name or contact details. The app doesn't access your location, photos or contacts. Users without a subscription see ads; section 4 explains what the ad network reads. There are no analytics tools.

1. Personal information we collect

None. No personal information — name, contact details, email, photos or anything else — ever reaches us. There are no accounts, so there is no sign-up or log-in.

However, to show ads, the ad network reads your device's advertising identifier and general device information directly from your device (section 4). We never receive or store those values.

Tapping Contact at the bottom of Settings shows our email address. Tapping the address opens your mail app, and the email you write is delivered to us. Your mail app sends it; the app itself collects nothing.

3. Payments, coins and subscriptions

Video stories and museum tours are unlocked with in-app coins. Coin packs and subscriptions (Member and Curator, monthly or yearly) are paid only through the App Store or Google Play. Payment details such as card numbers are handled directly by Apple and Google; we never see, receive or store them.

Subscriptions renew automatically for the same period unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends, and are charged to your store account. For plans with a free trial, the first charge is made when the trial ends. You can cancel at any time — on iPhone in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, on Android in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. On iPhone, Apple's Standard End User License Agreement (EULA) applies.

Coins received from a refunded purchase, and stories unlocked with those coins, are taken back.

4. Ads

Ads are shown only to users without a subscription — a banner at the bottom of the screen and a rewarded video ad that plays only when you tap "Watch an ad" yourself. Subscribing removes all ads.

  • Ad network — Ads are served through Unity's LevelPlay (formerly ironSource). Other ad networks mediated by LevelPlay may also participate.
  • What it reads — Advertising identifiers (IDFA/IDFV, Advertising ID), device information such as device model, OS, app version and language, approximate country/city inferred from your IP address, ad views and taps, and ad SDK diagnostics. The ad network reads these directly from your device; we never receive or store them.
  • What it doesn't get — The paintings you view, your favorites, and your coin and subscription records are not shared with the ad network.
  • Tracking consent — When a user without a subscription first opens the app, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt appears once. You can use every feature even if you decline, and change your choice anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland
When a user without a subscription first opens the app in these regions, the app asks whether the ad network may use your device for personalized ads — before any ad is requested. "Decline" is as easy as "Accept", and either way every feature works and ads still appear; declining just makes them non-personalized. We rely on your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) for personalized ads, and on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) for showing non-personalized ads. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy; it applies from the next ad request. On iPhone, the tracking prompt above appears after this question only if you accept; if you decline, it is not shown. The ad network may transfer data to the United States and other countries under its own safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses. Because we hold nothing that identifies you, requests to access or delete ad data are best made by resetting your advertising identifier or directly to the ad network.

The ad network's handling of data follows its own policy — Unity/LevelPlay Privacy Policy.

5. Every request the app sends

  • Paintings and stories (Cloudflare) — The app downloads the painting and story list, translations for your chosen language, and image files from our art host (museum-art.art-cdn.workers.dev, Cloudflare static files). These are downloads only; none of your data is sent. One request to the same host asks only for the current time, so subscription coins are given on the right date.
  • Version check — When the app starts, it reads one small file stating whether the current version may keep running and the ad settings (used to switch ads off in an emergency). It only reads; none of your data is sent.
  • App update check (Expo) — When the app starts, it asks Expo's update server whether a new screen update is available. No personal information is included.
  • Ad requests — Users without a subscription connect to the ad network to receive ads (section 4). After you subscribe, the app no longer connects to the ad network.
  • Store payments — The app talks to the App Store or Google Play on your device to show prices, make purchases and check your subscription.
  • Purchase notice (Android only) — When a purchase completes on Android, the app sends one request to our server (gwansang-server.onrender.com, hosted on Render) to tell the developer "one sold". It contains only the app name, platform, product ID, price and store order number — never your name, contact details or device identifiers. The server forwards it as one line to the developer's messenger (Telegram) and uses the order number only briefly to avoid duplicate notices, without storing it. iPhone never sends this request.

The servers these requests reach may log your IP address and access time; each provider handles those logs under its own policy. Sharing a painting only hands the image and an app link to your device's share sheet when you tap Share; the app sends nothing else.

6. Data stored only on your device

The downloaded painting list and image files, your coin ledger (purchased coins, subscription coins, unlocked stories), subscription tier, favorites, free reads, resume positions, chosen language, caption size, music and notification settings, and the Painting of the Day for the home screen widget. These stay on your device and are deleted when you delete the app. None of them identifies you.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, whether you accepted or declined personalized ads (section 4) is also stored on your device.

7. Sharing, international transfer and sale

We do not share or sell any information to third parties. Payments are processed by Apple and Google under their own policies. However, the values the ad network reads directly from your device (section 4) are transferred to that provider's servers abroad.

If you prefer, turn off tracking to stop the advertising identifier from being shared.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep no user data, so there is nothing to request deletion of. Values read by the ad network are kept and deleted under that provider's policy. Data on your device is deleted when you delete the app. Emails we receive are kept only as long as needed to reply, then deleted.

9. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If tracking is turned off, the advertising identifier is not used for personalized ads.

10. Sources of paintings and photos

The paintings in the app are works whose copyright has expired (public domain), in versions published on Wikimedia Commons. Venue photos in museum tours are freely licensed (CC BY and similar), with credits shown on screen.

11. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of your personal information. Since we hold no personal information, there is nothing to request, and you can delete the data on your device at any time by deleting the app.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date on this page. The revised policy takes effect on the day it is posted here.
