UNCLUE Privacy Policy

Effective: October 27, 2026 (revised)
Previous version effective: October 26, 2026
Contact: contact@nemofactory.co.kr

In one line
UNCLUE (formerly Catchphone) has no sign-up and no login, and does not ask for personal information such as your name or contact details. Your game progress is saved only on your device. However, the free version shows ads, and ad networks may read your device's advertising identifier (section 4). If you subscribe (UNCLUE PASS), no ads are shown, and payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play (section 5).

1. Personal information we collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, contact details, email or photos. There are no accounts, so there is no sign-up or login.

The phones, people, messages and photos in the game are all fiction. The app never reads what is on your real phone (photos, contacts or messages).

The advertising identifier is read by ad networks directly from your device. We neither receive nor keep it (section 4). Payment information (such as card numbers) is handled by the App Store or Google Play and never reaches us (section 5).

Tapping Contact at the bottom of the Settings tab shows our email address. Tapping the address opens the mail app on your device, and the email you write yourself comes to us. The subject line is pre-filled with the app version and device type (iOS or Android), which helps us reply.

3. Every request the app sends out

  • Version check: When the app starts, it reads one small file that says whether the current version may still be used (hosted by Netlify). It only reads; none of your values are sent.
  • App update check (Expo): When the app starts, it asks Expo's update server whether a new screen update is available. No personal information is sent; the server log may record your IP address.
  • Ads (versions with ads): The app communicates with ad networks to load and show ads. See section 4 for details.
  • Subscription payments: When you buy or restore a subscription, the app communicates with the App Store or Google Play. See section 5 for details.

Otherwise the app does not connect to the internet. Your game progress and endings never leave your device.

4. Ads

If you are not subscribed, the app shows ads: banners at the top or bottom of the screen, full-screen ads shown between questions as you solve a case (about every four correct answers) and once before the case result, and rewarded ads that you choose to watch yourself (to see a hint or unlock the next story).

  • Ad mediation: We use LevelPlay by Unity Technologies. LevelPlay picks which of several ad networks will show an ad, and those networks also communicate directly with your device. Currently these are Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize.
  • What ad networks read: the advertising identifier (on iPhone, the IDFA only if you allowed tracking; on Android, the advertising ID), your IP address, device information such as device type, OS version and language, and records of the ads you viewed or tapped. These are used to choose ads, avoid showing the same ad too often, and filter out fraudulent clicks.
  • Installed apps (Android): The ad networks’ code can check which apps are installed on your device. This is used to count whether you installed an app after seeing its ad, and to avoid showing ads again for apps you already have. UNCLUE does not read this, and it never reaches us — the app’s own code never asks for the list of installed apps; this happens inside the ad networks’ code. In Google Play’s “Data safety” section, we also declare App activity > Installed apps for advertising purposes.
  • These values are received directly by the ad networks and never reach us. Each network handles them under its own policy. Unity Privacy Policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • How to turn it off: On iPhone, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; on Android, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads. There you can turn off personalized ads or delete the advertising ID. If you subscribe, no ads are shown at all.

5. Subscription (UNCLUE PASS)

  • What it is: a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription. While subscribed, you can open every case and no ads are shown. It renews automatically at the end of each period, and you can cancel at any time in the subscription settings of the App Store or Google Play, up to 24 hours before the next payment.
  • Payment processing: Payments are handled by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). Payment information such as card numbers or addresses never reaches us.
  • Purchase check: The app checks whether you are subscribed using the purchase history returned by the store. On Android, it also verifies on the device, by its signature, that this history was genuinely issued by Google.
  • Sales notifications: To keep track of sales, on iPhone Apple sends purchase, renewal and refund notifications to our server, and on Android the app sends one line to our server (hosted by Render) at the moment a payment goes through. These contain only the product name, amount, store order number and device type, and no personal information such as your name, contact details or account. The notifications are used only by the operator to check sales.

6. What is stored only on your device

  • Game progress — progress for each case (the in-game phone clock, whether you unlocked it, your messages with the client, the questions you answered correctly and the ending), the time taken and the number of hints you viewed.
  • Settings — the language you chose and whether background music and sound effects are on.
  • Subscription status — whether you are subscribed. It is used only to decide whether to show ads and whether to open cases.
  • Ad-related records (versions with ads) — markers such as how many rewarded ads you have watched and whether a full-screen ad has already been shown.

All of the above stays on your device and is never sent to us. It is deleted when you delete the app. It contains nothing that identifies you.

8. Retention and deletion

The only things we keep are sales notifications (product name, amount and order number) and emails you send us. Sales notifications are kept for as long as needed for settlement and refund checks, and emails we receive are kept only as long as needed to reply, then deleted. Values on your device are deleted together with the app. Data held by ad networks is subject to those companies' policies.

9. Children under 14

UNCLUE is a game with adult stories such as investigating infidelity and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Music

The background music was composed and synthesized by us. No one else's recordings or samples were used.

11. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of your personal information under applicable privacy laws. The only data we hold are sales notifications and emails, and we will check and handle your request. You can delete the values on your device at any time by deleting the app, and you can turn off or delete the advertising identifier as described in sections 2 and 4.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date on this page. The revised policy takes effect on the day it is posted here.
