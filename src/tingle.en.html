Tingle Privacy Policy

Effective: October 30, 2026
Contact: contact@nemofactory.co.kr

In one line
Tingle has no sign-up and no login, and does not ask for personal information such as your name or contact details. Text you type with the Tingle keyboard is never saved and never sent anywhere. A rewarded ad plays only when you choose it yourself on the payment screen; what the ad network reads is described in section 5. There are no analytics.

1. Personal information we collect

We collect none. No personal information such as your name, contact details, email or photos ever reaches us. There are no accounts, so there is no sign-up or login.

However, ad networks read the device's advertising identifier and general device information directly from your device to show ads (section 5). We never receive or keep those values.

Tapping Contact at the bottom of the menu shows our email address. Tapping the address opens the mail app on your device, and the email you write yourself comes to us. Your mail app sends it; the app itself collects nothing.

2. Text you type with the keyboard

The Tingle keyboard only puts the letters you press into the field you are typing in and plays a sound when you press a key. The letters, words and sentences you type are not recorded, not sent off the device and never passed to us. There is no feature that collects text for spelling learning or autocomplete. The keyboard never connects to the internet, and there are no ads inside the keyboard. The "Recent" list on the emoji panel is stored only inside the keyboard.

3. Why "Allow Full Access" is turned on (iPhone)

On iPhone, iOS requires "Allow Full Access" for the Tingle keyboard to play sounds and to read the sound, keycap and language settings you chose in the app and your access status (subscription, free-use time). This permission is used only to play sounds and to share settings between the app and the keyboard; it is never used to connect to the internet or to send what you type. Typing works the same even if you leave it off.

5. Ads and payments

  • Ads appear in one place only — a rewarded video ad that plays only when you tap "Watch an ad for 6 hours" yourself on the payment screen. If you watch it to the end, you can use paid themes and sounds for 6 hours. There are no ads that pop up by themselves when you open the app or move between screens, and no banner ads. There are no ads inside the keyboard.
  • Ad network — ads are shown through Unity's LevelPlay (formerly ironSource). Other ad networks mediated by LevelPlay may also take part.
  • What they read — the advertising identifier (IDFA, IDFV / Advertising ID), device information such as device, OS, app version and language, approximate country and city inferred from your IP address, records of ads viewed and tapped, and logs of the ad code's behavior. Ad networks read these directly from your device; we never receive or keep them.
  • Installed apps (Android) — the advertising providers' code can check which apps are installed on the device, to count whether an ad led to an install and to avoid showing ads for apps you already have. Tingle does not read this, and it never reaches us — nowhere in the app's own code asks for the installed-app list; this happens inside the advertising providers' code.
  • What is not sent — the text you type and the sounds, keycaps and settings you choose are never passed to ad networks.
  • Tracking consent — the first time a user who has not made a purchase opens the app, Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt appears once. You can decline and still use every feature. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland
When a user who has not made a purchase first opens the app in these regions, it asks whether the ad networks above (Unity, Liftoff) may use your device for personalized ads — before any ad is requested. "Decline" is as easy as "Accept", and either way every feature works and ads still appear; declining just makes them non-personalized. We rely on your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) for personalized ads, and on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) for showing non-personalized ads and for the ad misuse guard (section 7). You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in Menu (☰) > Ad privacy; it applies from the next ad request. On iPhone, if you decline, the tracking (ATT) prompt above does not appear; if you accept, it appears after this question. The ad networks may transfer data to the United States and other countries under their own safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses. Because we hold nothing that identifies you, requests to access or delete ad data are best made by resetting your advertising identifier or directly to the ad networks above.

Ad networks handle data under their own policies — Unity/LevelPlay Privacy Policy.

Payments are handled by the App Store and Google Play. Subscriptions (monthly, yearly) and lifetime access are paid only through the store, and payment details such as card numbers go straight to Apple and Google. We never see, receive or keep them.

Subscriptions renew automatically for the same period unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends, and are charged to your store account. You can cancel any time on iPhone in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, and on Android in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. On iPhone, terms of use follow Apple's standard License Agreement (EULA).

6. Every request the app sends out

  • Keyboard: none.
  • Version check: when the app opens, it reads one small file that says whether the current version can still be used, along with ad settings (a value used when ads must be switched off urgently). It is read-only and carries none of your data.
  • App update check (Expo): when the app opens, it asks the Expo update server whether a new screen update is available. No personal information is included; your IP address may remain in that server's logs.
  • Ad requests: so that an ad plays as soon as you tap the button, users who have not made a purchase connect to the ad network when the app opens and load an ad in advance (section 5). After you make a purchase, the app no longer connects to the ad network.
  • Store payments: the app talks to the App Store or Google Play on your device to show prices and make purchases.
  • Purchase notification (Android only): on Android, the moment a payment completes, the app sends one request to our server (gwansang-server.onrender.com, hosted on Render) to tell the developer "one was sold." It carries only the app name, platform, product ID, amount and store order number; no name, contact details, device identifier or typed text. The server forwards this as one line to the developer's messenger (Telegram), uses the order number only briefly to avoid sending the same notification twice, and does not store it. This request is not sent on iPhone.

7. What is stored on your device only

Your chosen sound, keycap color and shape, keyboard layout (Korean Dubeolsik, Cheonjiin, Danmoeum), vibration and key-release sound on/off, chosen language, access status (when a subscription, lifetime access or free use ends), your personalized-ads consent choice (whether you accepted or declined in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), and an ad misuse guard record (a few timestamps used to pause ads briefly if they are tapped repeatedly in a short time). These stay only on the device (in storage shared by the app and the keyboard) and are deleted when you delete the app. None of them identifies you.

8. Sharing, international transfer, sale

We pass on or sell no information to third parties. Payments are processed directly by Apple and Google under their own policies. However, the values ad networks read directly from your device (section 5) are sent to those companies' servers abroad.

9. Retention and deletion

We keep no user data, so there is nothing for you to ask us to delete. Values read by ad networks are kept and deleted under their own policies. Values on your device are deleted when you delete the app. Emails received through Contact are kept only as long as needed to reply, then deleted.

10. Children under 14

We do not knowingly collect children's personal information. If tracking is turned off, the advertising identifier is not used for personalized ads.

11. Sources of sounds and images

Keyboard sounds are freely licensed recordings (CC0, CC BY) and sounds we synthesized ourselves; their sources are listed on the "Sound credits" screen in the app.

12. Your rights

Under applicable privacy laws you may request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of your personal information. However, we hold no personal information, so there is nothing to request; you can delete the values on your device yourself at any time by deleting the app.

For users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

This section applies if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland. It adds to the sections above.

  • Who is responsible — Nemo Factory (Republic of Korea) is the controller for the little we process ourselves. Contact: contact@nemofactory.co.kr. The Republic of Korea is covered by adequacy decisions of the European Commission and the United Kingdom.
  • What we process and on what basis — data kept only on your device is not processed by us. If you email us, we use your message only to answer you (our legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and keep it only as long as described above. Purchases are handled by Apple and Google as separate controllers; the app checks your purchase with the store to unlock what you bought (performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Ads — personalised ads are shown only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)); non-personalised ads and protection against ad fraud rely on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)). The ad networks named above process this data as independent controllers under their own policies. You can withdraw your consent at any time with the ad privacy option in the app described above; withdrawal does not affect processing before it.
  • Your rights — you can ask for access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, and you can object to processing based on our legitimate interest. Email us and we will reply within one month. Because we hold almost nothing that identifies you, we may not be able to find data linked to you. For advertising data, reset your device's advertising identifier or contact the ad networks directly.
  • No automated decisions — we make no decisions with legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing.
  • Children — if you are under the age at which your country requires a parent's consent for online services (between 13 and 16 in the EEA, 13 in the UK), do not accept personalised ads without a parent's permission. If we learn that we hold a child's data without such consent, we delete it.
  • Transfers — the ad networks, Apple and Google may transfer data outside your country, including to the United States, under their own safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Complaints — you can complain to the data protection authority of the country where you live or work.
