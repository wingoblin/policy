1. Personal information we collect

Deep Galaxy is operated by Nemo factory (네모팩토리), Republic of Korea; representative and privacy contact: Yang Seongmin (양성민). There are no accounts, sign-in or separate usage analytics tools. We process Android purchase notifications and request logs (section 5), and your email address, message and attachments when you contact us. Ad SDKs collect identifiers, device information, approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostics (section 4). We do not receive card numbers or passwords.

2. What is stored on your device only

The device stores language, music, viewing mode, vehicle, Pro status, ad consent, misuse protection, remote settings, first launch and tutorial flags, and launch days, visited celestial-body IDs and app version for rating requests. These usage records are not sent to our server. Clear them in Android Settings → Apps → Deep Galaxy → Storage → Clear data. Uninstalling removes local data, but OS backup restoration may restore settings. Store purchase history remains separate.

4. Ads and payments

  • Ads appear in one place only — a rewarded video ad that plays only when you choose "Watch an ad" yourself on the way to a free destination (the Sun, Mercury, Venus, Earth, the Moon, Mars, Jupiter and Jupiter's moons). If you watch it to the end, you go there. There are no ads that pop up by themselves when you open the app or move between screens, and no banner ads.
  • Ad network — ads are shown through Unity's LevelPlay (formerly ironSource); the ad partners are Unity Ads, Liftoff and Meta Audience Network.
  • What they read — the advertising identifier (IDFA, IDFV / Android Advertising ID, App Set ID), device information such as device, OS, app version and language, approximate country and city inferred from your IP address, records of ads viewed and tapped, and logs of the ad code's behavior. Ad networks read these directly from your device; we never receive or keep them.
  • Installed apps (Android) — the advertising providers' code can check which apps are installed on the device, to count whether an ad led to an install and to avoid showing ads for apps you already have.
  • Tracking consent — the first time a user who has not bought Space Pro opens the app, Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt appears once. If you do not allow it, you see non-personalized ads and can still use every feature. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Turning off the Android Advertising ID — Android has no tracking prompt. If you do not want personalized ads, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (on some devices Settings → Google → Ads) and delete or reset your Advertising ID. Once it is deleted, ad networks receive only a string of zeros instead of the ID, and every feature keeps working.
  • After you buy Space Pro — the ad features (the ad networks' code) are not turned on, and the tracking prompt is not shown.

Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland
In these regions, the app first asks inside the app whether the ad partners above (Unity, Liftoff, Meta) may use your device for personalized ads — before any ad is requested. "Decline" is as easy as "Accept", and either way every feature works and ads still appear; declining just makes them non-personalized. We rely on your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) for personalized ads, and on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) for showing non-personalized ads and for the ad misuse guard (section 2). You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the app's Settings > Ad privacy; it applies from the next ad request. On iPhone, the tracking (ATT) prompt comes first, and this question follows only if you allow tracking; if you do not, only non-personalized ads are shown without this question. The ad networks may transfer data to the United States and other countries under their own safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

Ad networks handle data under their own policies — Unity/LevelPlay Privacy Policy · Liftoff Privacy Policy · Meta Privacy Policy.

Space Pro is a one-time purchase that lasts for life (it is not a subscription and never renews). Payments are handled by the App Store and Google Play, and payment details such as card numbers go straight to Apple and Google. We never see, receive or keep them. When you restore a purchase, the app only checks with the store on your device whether you bought it. On iPhone, terms of use follow Apple's standard License Agreement (EULA).

5. Every request the app sends out

  • App status file — when the app starts, it reads one small file on our website (hosted on Netlify) to check whether your version must be updated, along with ad settings (a value used when ads must be switched off urgently). The request only downloads the file and carries no values about you. As with any web page, the hosting server's logs may record the connecting IP address. Without a connection the check is skipped.
  • Ad requests — for users who have not bought Space Pro, the app connects to the ad network when it opens and loads an ad in advance when the window where you can choose an ad opens (section 4). After you buy Space Pro, the app no longer connects to the ad network.
  • Store payments — the app talks to the App Store or Google Play on your device to show prices, make purchases and restore them.
  • Purchase notification (Android only) — on Android, the moment a payment completes, the app sends one request to our server to tell the developer "one was sold." It carries only the app name, platform, product (name and ID), amount, time of payment and purchase token (the value Google Play attaches to this payment); no name, contact details or device identifier. The server forwards this as one line to the developer's messenger. The purchase token is used only briefly to avoid sending the same notification twice and is not stored separately. The server's logs may record part of the connecting IP address, information about the connecting software and the beginning of the purchase token. This request is not sent on iPhone.
  • Links you tap yourself — "Rate the app" (the App Store or Google Play), "Privacy Policy" (this page, GitHub Pages) and "Contact" (your mail app) open on your device; from there that service's own policy applies.

All images and star data are inside the app, so exploring works without the internet.

7. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until cleared. Contact emails are kept while needed to answer and resolve the issue, then deleted. Purchase-token deduplication uses server memory, with up to 500 entries, evicted oldest-first or cleared on restart. This does not delete server logs or messenger notifications. Logs serve operations and security; notifications serve purchase checks and support while needed. Provider records also follow their retention and deletion policies. Legally required records remain for the required period. Email us to request deletion of data we hold. Resetting an ad ID, uninstalling or buying Pro does not erase transmitted data. Request ad-data deletion from ad providers; we can help direct your request. App-status and purchase-notification requests use HTTPS.

8. Images and data

Planet and moon maps and star data come from Solar System Scope (CC BY 4.0), NASA and USGS (public domain) and the HYG Database (CC BY-SA 4.0). Full credits are in the app under Settings › Credits.
