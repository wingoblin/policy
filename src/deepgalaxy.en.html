Deep Galaxy — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 12, 2026
Contact: contact@nemofactory.co.kr

In one line. Deep Galaxy has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no server that stores your information. The planets, moons, stars and black hole are drawn on your device from images built into the app. The only internet request the app makes on its own is checking an app status file (section 5).

1. Personal information we collect

None. We do not collect your name, email, contact details, location, payment details or any other personal information. There is no account feature, so there is no sign-up or sign-in.

2. What is stored on your device

Nothing about you. The app does not save your views, history or settings. It follows your device's language.

3. Permissions

The app asks for no permissions. It does not access the camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts, calendars or health data. It does not read the advertising identifier and never shows a tracking prompt.

4. Ads and analytics

There are no ads, and no analytics or crash-reporting tools are included. We do not collect how you use the app.

5. Every request the app sends out

  • App status file — when the app starts, it reads one small file on our website (hosted on Netlify) to check whether your version must be updated. The request only downloads the file and carries no values about you. As with any web page, the hosting server's logs may record the connecting IP address. Without a connection the check is skipped.
  • Links you tap yourself — "Rate the app" (the App Store or Google Play), "Privacy Policy" (this page, GitHub Pages) and "Contact" (your mail app) open on your device; from there that service's own policy applies.

All images and star data are inside the app, so exploring works without the internet.

6. Images and data

Planet and moon maps and star data come from Solar System Scope (CC BY 4.0), NASA and USGS (public domain) and the HYG Database (CC BY-SA 4.0). Full credits are in the app under Settings › Credits.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
