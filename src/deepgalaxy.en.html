Deep Galaxy — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 12, 2026
Contact: contact@nemofactory.co.kr

In one line. Deep Galaxy has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no server that stores your information. The planets, moons, stars and black hole are drawn on your device from images built into the app. A rewarded ad plays only when you choose it yourself on the way to a free destination; what the ad network reads is described in section 4. If you buy Space Pro, the ad features are never turned on.

1. Personal information we collect

We collect none. No personal information such as your name, email, contact details, location or payment details ever reaches us. There is no account feature, so there is no sign-up or sign-in.

However, ad networks read the device's advertising identifier and general device information directly from your device to show ads (section 4). We never receive or keep those values.

Tapping Contact in Settings opens the mail app on your device, and the email you write yourself comes to us. Your mail app sends it; the app itself collects nothing.

2. What is stored on your device only

The language you chose in the app, whether you bought Space Pro, your personalized-ads consent choice (whether you accepted or declined in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), an ad misuse guard record (a few timestamps used to pause ads briefly if they are tapped repeatedly in a short time), and a copy of the ad settings from the app status file. These stay only on the device and are deleted when you delete the app. None of them identifies you. The app does not save which objects you viewed or your browsing history.

4. Ads and payments

  • Ads appear in one place only — a rewarded video ad that plays only when you choose "Watch an ad" yourself on the way to a free destination (the Sun, Mercury, Venus, Earth, the Moon, Mars, Jupiter and Jupiter's moons). If you watch it to the end, you go there. There are no ads that pop up by themselves when you open the app or move between screens, and no banner ads.
  • Ad network — ads are shown through Unity's LevelPlay (formerly ironSource); the ad partners are Unity Ads and Liftoff.
  • What they read — the advertising identifier (IDFA, IDFV / Advertising ID), device information such as device, OS, app version and language, approximate country and city inferred from your IP address, records of ads viewed and tapped, and logs of the ad code's behavior. Ad networks read these directly from your device; we never receive or keep them.
  • Installed apps (Android) — the advertising providers' code can check which apps are installed on the device, to count whether an ad led to an install and to avoid showing ads for apps you already have. Deep Galaxy does not read this, and it never reaches us.
  • Tracking consent — the first time a user who has not bought Space Pro opens the app, Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt appears once. If you do not allow it, you see non-personalized ads and can still use every feature. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • After you buy Space Pro — the ad features (the ad networks' code) are not turned on, and the tracking prompt is not shown.

Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland
In these regions, the app first asks inside the app whether the ad partners above (Unity, Liftoff) may use your device for personalized ads — before any ad is requested. "Decline" is as easy as "Accept", and either way every feature works and ads still appear; declining just makes them non-personalized. We rely on your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) for personalized ads, and on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) for showing non-personalized ads and for the ad misuse guard (section 2). You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the app's Settings > Ad privacy; it applies from the next ad request. On iPhone, the tracking (ATT) prompt comes first, and this question follows only if you allow tracking; if you do not, only non-personalized ads are shown without this question. The ad networks may transfer data to the United States and other countries under their own safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses. Because we hold nothing that identifies you, requests to access or delete ad data are best made by resetting your advertising identifier or directly to the ad networks above.

Ad networks handle data under their own policies — Unity/LevelPlay Privacy Policy · Liftoff Privacy Policy.

Space Pro is a one-time purchase that lasts for life (it is not a subscription and never renews). Payments are handled by the App Store and Google Play, and payment details such as card numbers go straight to Apple and Google. We never see, receive or keep them. When you restore a purchase, the app only checks with the store on your device whether you bought it. On iPhone, terms of use follow Apple's standard License Agreement (EULA).

5. Every request the app sends out

  • App status file — when the app starts, it reads one small file on our website (hosted on Netlify) to check whether your version must be updated, along with ad settings (a value used when ads must be switched off urgently). The request only downloads the file and carries no values about you. As with any web page, the hosting server's logs may record the connecting IP address. Without a connection the check is skipped.
  • Ad requests — for users who have not bought Space Pro, the app connects to the ad network when it opens and loads an ad in advance when the window where you can choose an ad opens (section 4). After you buy Space Pro, the app no longer connects to the ad network.
  • Store payments — the app talks to the App Store or Google Play on your device to show prices, make purchases and restore them.
  • Purchase notification (Android only) — on Android, the moment a payment completes, the app sends one request to our server (gwansang-server.onrender.com, hosted on Render) to tell the developer "one was sold." It carries only the app name, platform, product ID, amount and store order number; no name, contact details or device identifier. The server forwards this as one line to the developer's messenger (Telegram), uses the order number only briefly to avoid sending the same notification twice, and does not store it. This request is not sent on iPhone.
  • Links you tap yourself — "Rate the app" (the App Store or Google Play), "Privacy Policy" (this page, GitHub Pages) and "Contact" (your mail app) open on your device; from there that service's own policy applies.

All images and star data are inside the app, so exploring works without the internet.

6. Sharing, international transfer, sale

We pass on or sell no information to third parties. Payments are processed directly by Apple and Google under their own policies. However, the values ad networks read directly from your device (section 4) are sent to those companies' servers abroad.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep no user data, so there is nothing for you to ask us to delete. Values read by ad networks are kept and deleted under their own policies. Values on your device are deleted when you delete the app. Emails received through Contact are kept only as long as needed to reply, then deleted.

8. Images and data

Planet and moon maps and star data come from Solar System Scope (CC BY 4.0), NASA and USGS (public domain) and the HYG Database (CC BY-SA 4.0). Full credits are in the app under Settings › Credits.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information. If tracking is turned off, the advertising identifier is not used for personalized ads.

10. Your rights

Under applicable privacy laws you may request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of your personal information. However, we hold no personal information, so there is nothing to request; you can delete the values on your device yourself at any time by deleting the app.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date on this page. The revised policy takes effect on the day it is posted on this page.

For users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

This section applies if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland. It adds to the sections above.

  • Who is responsible — the developer of this app (Republic of Korea) is the controller for the little we process ourselves. Contact: contact@nemofactory.co.kr. The Republic of Korea is covered by adequacy decisions of the European Commission and the United Kingdom.
  • What we process and on what basis — data kept only on your device is not processed by us. If you email us, we use your message only to answer you (our legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and keep it only as long as described above. Purchases are handled by Apple and Google as separate controllers; the app checks your purchase with the store to unlock what you bought (performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Ads — personalised ads are shown only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)); non-personalised ads and protection against ad fraud rely on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)). The ad networks named above process this data as independent controllers under their own policies. You can withdraw your consent at any time with the Ad privacy option in the app's Settings described above; withdrawal does not affect processing before it.
  • Your rights — you can ask for access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, and you can object to processing based on our legitimate interest. Email us and we will reply within one month. Because we hold almost nothing that identifies you, we may not be able to find data linked to you. For advertising data, reset your device's advertising identifier or contact the ad networks directly.
  • No automated decisions — we make no decisions with legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing.
  • Children — if you are under the age at which your country requires a parent's consent for online services (between 13 and 16 in the EEA, 13 in the UK), do not accept personalised ads without a parent's permission. If we learn that we hold a child's data without such consent, we delete it.
  • Transfers — the ad networks, Apple and Google may transfer data outside your country, including to the United States, under their own safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Complaints — you can complain to the data protection authority of the country where you live or work.
