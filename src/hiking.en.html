Let's Hike Korea — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 5, 2026

Contact: koreatong.kr@gmail.com

In one line
Let's Hike Korea collects no personal data. There is no account and no sign-in.

Not a government app
Let's Hike Korea is made by an independent developer. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Korea Forest Service, the Korea National Park Service, or any other government body. Mountain information and wildfire-caution dates shown in the app are compiled by the app from public open data and are not an official notice. Check the original sources below.
Sup-nadeul-e · Korea Forest Service · Korea National Park Service · Public Data Portal

1. Personal data we collect

None. We collect no name, contact details, email, or device identifier. Location is used only at the moment you stamp a summit and is not stored (section 3). The app has no accounts, so there is no sign-up or sign-in at all.

2. Stored on your device only

  • Your saved mountains — so you can find them again. Deleting the app removes them.
  • How many mountains you opened today — used for the daily viewing limit. It resets at midnight.

Neither leaves the device. Nothing is stored on a server and we cannot read it.

3. Location

Your location is checked once, only when you stamp a summit, to decide whether you are near that mountain’s trail. The coordinates are neither stored on the device nor sent anywhere. The check happens on your phone, and all that remains is the fact that the stamp was given.

Declining the location permission changes nothing else about the app.

If your phone could not get a fix on the mountain, picking a photo you took that day lets the app read the capture coordinates (EXIF) stored inside that photo and run the same check. Those coordinates are used for the check only and are not kept, and nothing is read unless you pick the photo yourself.

4. Requests the app makes

  • OpenStreetMap — map tiles for the location preview.
  • Wikimedia Commons — mountain photos.
  • unpkg — the map library (Leaflet) used to draw the map view.
  • Developer site — sends only the installed app version, to check the minimum supported version.

None of these carry anything that identifies you. Each service may process connection data (such as IP addresses) under its own policy in order to serve the request.

5. Advertising

Outside the Korean-language experience there are no banner ads. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one to raise your daily viewing limit. In the Korean-language experience a bottom banner is also shown.

Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay mediation. Ad providers may use your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, iOS IDFA) and coarse connection data for personalisation and frequency capping. We neither collect nor store that identifier, and nothing the app saves (your saved mountains and so on) is passed to advertisers.

  • Android — Settings > Google > Ads lets you delete the advertising ID or opt out of personalisation.
  • iOS — the app asks for tracking permission on first launch. Declining changes nothing about how the app works, and you can change it any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

6. Sharing

Apart from the ad providers above, no personal data is shared with or processed by third parties.

7. Your rights

Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. No separate deletion request is needed.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated together with a new effective date.
