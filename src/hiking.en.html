Let's Hike Korea — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026 (revised)
Previous version: October 10, 2026

Contact: contact@nemofactory.co.kr

In one line
Let's Hike Korea collects no personal data. There is no account and no sign-in.

Not a government app
Let's Hike Korea is made by an independent developer. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Korea Forest Service, the Korea National Park Service, or any other government body. Mountain information and wildfire-caution dates shown in the app are compiled by the app from public open data and are not an official notice. Check the original sources below.
Sup-nadeul-e · Korea Forest Service · Korea National Park Service · Public Data Portal

1. Personal data we collect

None. We collect no name, contact details, email, or device identifier. Location is used only for summit verification, and the spot and time of a verified summit stay on your device only (section 3). The app has no accounts, so there is no sign-up or sign-in at all.

2. Stored on your device only

  • Your saved mountains — so you can find them again.
  • Your hiking records — date, note and photos, plus the spot (coordinates) and time of a verified summit. Photos you pick are copied into the app folder and nothing more.
  • Wildfire-caution notice and mountain forecast — the last copy fetched is kept so it can be reused.
  • Certificate settings — the certificate design you picked, and a mark for any design you bought.
  • Ad and review-request records — counts and times used to keep ads from showing too often, and when we last asked for a review.
  • App settings — such as the display language.

None of it leaves the device. Nothing is stored on a server and we cannot read it. Deleting the app removes all of it.

3. Location

Only when you tap verify summit, your location is checked several times over a few seconds to decide, on your phone, whether you are standing near that summit. If the summit is verified, one of the points used (coordinates) and the time are saved with that mountain’s record. They are stored on your device only and are never sent anywhere. If the summit is not verified, no location is kept.

Declining the location permission changes nothing else about the app.

When you add a photo to a record, the app reads the capture coordinates and time (EXIF) inside that photo only to tell whether it was taken near that mountain’s trail (it is not used for summit verification). Those coordinates are used for the check only and are not kept, and nothing is read unless you pick the photo yourself.

4. Requests the app makes

  • Korea Tourism Organization — mountain photos (images from its tourism information service).
  • The map built into your phone — the map view uses your phone's own map (Apple Maps on iPhone, Google Maps on Android). When a map is shown, that map provider fetches it from its own servers.
  • Korea Meteorological Administration API Hub — the mountain weather forecast on a mountain page. Only that mountain’s forecast-point number and the issue time are sent.
  • Developer site — used to check the minimum supported version and to update the wildfire-caution dates and ad settings. Only the installed app version is sent.
  • Payments (App Store, Google Play) — if you buy a certificate design, Apple or Google handles the payment. Card and payment details never reach us.
  • Payment alert (Android) — when a purchase completes, the app name, product, amount and order number are sent to the developer’s server so we get a payment alert. No name, email or account is sent. The server does not store it; only the start of the order number and the first two parts of the IP address briefly remain in the server log.

Apart from the payment alert, none of these carry anything that identifies you. Each service may process connection data (such as IP addresses) under its own policy in order to serve the request.

5. Advertising

A bottom banner is shown. When you open a mountain page, a rectangle ad appears on the loading panel while its photos download, and from the fourth mountain page you open, a full-screen interstitial ad appears once per app run (a short notice comes first). This is the same in every language. Mountain information is never locked behind an ad. Saving a summit certificate from the Records tab asks first whether you want to watch a rewarded video, and one plays only if you agree (if no ad can be loaded it simply goes ahead, and saving with a design you bought never asks).

Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay mediation. Ad providers may use your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, iOS IDFA) and coarse connection data for personalisation and frequency capping. We neither collect nor store that identifier, and nothing the app saves (your saved mountains and so on) is passed to advertisers.

Installed apps (Android) — the ad providers' code may check which apps are installed on your device, to count installs that came from an ad and to avoid showing ads for apps you already have. Let's Hike Korea does not read this and it never reaches us — the app's own code never asks for the installed-app list; this happens inside the ad providers' code. We have also declared App activity > Installed apps for advertising in Google Play's Data safety section.

  • Android — Settings > Google > Ads lets you delete the advertising ID or opt out of personalisation.
  • iOS — the app may ask for tracking permission in order to personalise ads. Declining changes nothing about how the app works, and you can change it any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

6. Sharing

Apart from the ad providers above, no personal data is shared with or processed by third parties. The ad provider (Unity), the map and payment providers (Apple, Google) and the host of the payment-alert server (Render) are companies outside Korea; what they process is limited to what is described in sections 4 and 5. We never transfer your personal data abroad ourselves.

7. Retention

We collect no personal data, so there is no retention period. Everything in section 2 stays on your device and disappears the moment you delete the app. The payment-alert server log (section 4) is not collected separately and disappears when the host’s log retention ends.

8. Security

Apart from the payment alert (section 4), this app has no server that receives your data. Not creating a place for a leak to happen is this app's security measure. Every request the app makes (section 4) goes over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

9. Your rights

Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. No separate deletion request is needed. Location and photo permissions can be withdrawn at any time in your device settings, and declining them changes nothing else about the app.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

If you need help with a privacy matter, you may contact the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (kopico.go.kr) or the Korea Internet & Security Agency privacy report centre (privacy.kisa.or.kr).

13. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the effective date on this page. The revised policy takes effect on the day it is posted here.
