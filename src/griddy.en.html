Griddy — Support & Privacy Policy

App: Griddy  ·  Developer: Nemo Factory  ·  Effective: October 21, 2026

In one line. Griddy has no sign-up and keeps your plans, journal, photos and settings on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync on iOS (off by default), your plans, journal and photos are also stored in your own iCloud (section 3). Ads are shown while you use Griddy for free, and buying the unlock removes them (section 4).

Support

How do I unlock the extra fonts and colors?
Open the ☰ menu in the top-left corner, go to Customize, then tap any dimmed font. The unlock sheet opens with the purchase button. Locked colors work the same way: open any event, tap + at the end of the color row, then tap a dimmed color. In the journal, tapping any tape, paper or text color with a lock opens the same sheet.

I already paid, but the app asks again.
Open the same unlock sheet and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you are signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you bought it with.

How do I remove ads?
Buying the unlock removes all ads. You can buy it from the same unlock sheet, and it is a single one-time payment.

Is it a subscription?
No. It is a single one-time purchase. There is nothing to cancel and nothing renews.

My widget is not updating.
Home screen widgets are refreshed by the operating system, so there can be a short delay. Opening the app updates them right away.

Where is my data if I change phones?
By default everything is stored on the device only, so it does not move with you. If you turned on iCloud sync on iPhone or iPad, your plans, journal and photos follow you to a new device on the same Apple Account. Without iCloud, or on Android, use Export backup in Settings, then Import backup on the new device. Deleting the app deletes the data on the device with it.

Still stuck? Email contact@nemofactory.co.kr and include your device model and OS version. We reply in English, Korean, or Japanese.

Privacy Policy

1. In one line

Griddy has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server that collects your data. Your plans, journal, photos and settings live on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync on iOS, your plans, journal and photos are also stored in your own iCloud, which likewise never passes through our servers (section 3). However, ads are shown while you use the app for free, and in delivering them our ad partners process your device's advertising identifier and related information (section 4). We use no analytics tools.

2. Information we collect

Nemo Factory collects nothing. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, location or contacts, and the events, journal entries and photos you add are never sent to us.

For ad delivery, however, our ad partners process the information listed in section 4 directly on your device. This information does not pass through our servers, and Nemo Factory does not keep it.

One more exception: tapping Contact at the bottom of Settings opens the mail app on your device. What you write, the reply address (if you add one) and your device OS and version travel in that email to us. Your mail app sends it — the app itself collects nothing and sends nothing to our servers.

3. What is stored on your device, and iCloud sync

  • Events and categories — title, time, colour, done state, weekly repeat
  • Journal — date, title, text, decorations (tape, paper, lettering) and the photos you add (copies of the photos you pick)
  • Checklists, templates and saved titles
  • Appearance settings — row size, alignment, start mark, grid style, palette, font, clock format, theme
  • Unlock purchase state — the result of the store receipt check (the fact of purchase only; no payment details)
  • Ad protection record (ads.guard.v1) — when an ad was last shown and how many times ads were tapped. Used to prevent invalid clicks; it does not identify you
  • Copy of ad settings (ads.remote.v1) — the ad on/off and ad unit settings fetched from the app status file
  • Personalized ad consent (ads.gdpr.v1, Europe only) — the answer you chose in the consent dialog

All of it lives in the app's local database and storage and is deleted with the app.

iCloud sync (iOS only, off by default). When you turn it on in Settings, your events, category names, journal (including photos), checklists and templates are stored in your own private iCloud database and kept in step with your other devices on the same Apple Account. Appearance settings, purchase state and ad records are not uploaded. The data sits in storage Apple provides — it never passes through Nemo Factory's servers, and we cannot see its contents. Turning it off stops further uploads, and you can delete what is already there in Settings › Apple Account › iCloud on your device. Android does not have this feature.

Backup files. When you tap Export backup in Settings, a backup file (.griddy) holding your events, journal, photos, checklists and templates is created on your device and handed to the share sheet, where you choose where it goes (Files, a messenger, and so on). It never passes through Nemo Factory's servers; once sent, it is kept under the policy of the service you chose. Import backup reads only the file you pick.

4. Ads

While you use the app for free, ads appear in three places. Once you buy the unlock, no ads are shown at all.

  • The lower part of the brief screen shown during a transition — when switching between the daily and weekly planners, and when moving from the journal calendar to the continuous journal view
  • Interstitial ad — once each time you open the app, when you return to the calendar after writing in the journal. Right before it, the app tells you “You'll see just one ad”
  • A banner at the bottom of the Templates screen

No ads are placed on the planner grid or between list items.

Ads are delivered through Unity Technologies' LevelPlay mediation, with Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize (Vungle) supplying the ads.

Information processed for ads (processed directly on your device by the ad partners):

  • Advertising identifier — IDFA on iOS (if you allow tracking), Advertising ID (AAID) on Android
  • Device and app information — device model, OS version, app version, language, time zone, screen and connection information
  • Approximate location — country or region estimated from your IP address (precise location is never requested)
  • Ad interactions — whether an ad was shown or tapped, and ad performance information
  • Installed apps (Android) — the ad partners' SDKs may check which apps are installed on the device for ad delivery and fraud prevention
  • Ad code activity logs — whether ad requests succeeded and how long they took. This is diagnostics for the ad code itself, not a collection of Griddy's error logs
  • Ad placement usage records — when the app was opened and when an ad was shown

In the App Store "App Privacy" section and the Google Play "Data safety" section, we have declared the items above for advertising purposes, and marked the advertising identifier and ad interactions as used for "tracking". This does not mean we receive these values — it means the ad partners process them.

Personalized ads and your choices

  • Europe (EEA, UK and Switzerland) — before any ads start, the app asks for your consent to personalized ads. Decline and Accept sit side by side at the same size, and if you decline you can still use every feature of the app; you will see non-personalized ads instead. You can change your answer at any time under Ad privacy in Settings. Personalized ads are based on your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)); non-personalized ads and invalid-click protection are based on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)). The ad partners may transfer information to the United States and other countries under their own safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • iOS — when you first open the app, it asks whether to allow tracking (App Tracking Transparency). If you declined personalized ads in Europe, this question is not asked. The app works the same if you don't allow it, and you can change it later in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android — in Settings › Privacy › Ads you can delete your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads.

Ad partners' privacy policies: Unity Technologies · Liftoff Monetize

5. Permissions

  • Notifications — to show an event's start reminder on your device
  • Exact alarms — so reminders fire at the right minute
  • Photos — used only when you pick photos for a journal entry. Only the photos you pick are copied into the app; nothing else is read
  • Widgets — to show today's plan on the home and lock screens
  • Tracking (iOS) — used only for personalized ads. The app works the same if you don't allow it
  • Advertising ID (Android) — used only for ad delivery

Every permission can be turned off in system settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.

6. Sharing and third parties

Because we collect nothing, we have nothing to share with third parties. For ad delivery only, the ad partners in section 4 process information directly on your device, and each partner's own policy applies. We use no analytics tools.

7. Payments

The unlock is sold through App Store / Google Play in-app purchase. Nemo Factory never receives or stores card numbers or other payment details — only the fact that a purchase exists, stored on your device. Once you buy the unlock, no ads are shown.

8. Storage and deletion

Everything is stored on your device (apart from iCloud sync you turn on and backup files you export yourself) and removed when you delete the app. We hold no user data, so there is nothing for us to erase. To delete information held by the ad partners, reset your advertising ID as described in section 4.

Emails you send through Contact are kept only as long as we need them to reply and fix the problem, then deleted.

9. Children's privacy

Griddy is not directed to children, and we do not receive identifying information from anyone, children included. Guardians may contact us with any question about a child's use.

10. Your choices

Delete an event or journal entry, or delete the app, to remove your data immediately. You can turn off personalized ads at any time as described in section 4. For any other question, email us at the address below.

11. Changes and contact

When this policy changes, we update the effective date on this page. The changed policy takes effect from the day it is posted on this page. Contact: contact@nemofactory.co.kr (Nemo Factory)
