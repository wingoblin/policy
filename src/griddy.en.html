Griddy — Support & Privacy Policy

App: Griddy  ·  Developer: Nemo Factory  ·  Effective: September 17, 2026

In one line. Griddy has no sign-up and keeps your plans and settings on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync on iOS (off by default), your plans are also stored in your own iCloud (section 3). There are no ads and no analytics.

Support

How do I unlock the extra fonts and colors?
Tap the … button in the top-right corner, open Customize, then tap any dimmed font in the Font row. The unlock sheet opens with the purchase button. Locked colors work the same way: open any event, tap + at the end of the color row, then tap a dimmed color.

I already paid, but the app asks again.
Open the same unlock sheet and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you are signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you bought it with.

Is it a subscription?
No. It is a single one-time purchase. There is nothing to cancel and nothing renews.

My widget is not updating.
Home screen widgets are refreshed by the operating system, so there can be a short delay. Opening the app updates them right away.

Where is my data if I change phones?
By default everything is stored on the device only, so it does not move with you. If you turned on iCloud sync on iPhone or iPad, your plans do follow you to a new device on the same Apple Account. Deleting the app deletes the data on the device with it.

Still stuck? Email contact@nemofactory.co.kr and include your device model and OS version. We reply in English, Korean, or Japanese.

Privacy Policy

1. In one line

Griddy has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server that collects your data. Your plans and settings live on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync on iOS, your plans are also stored in your own iCloud, which likewise never passes through our servers (section 3). We use no advertising and no analytics (tracking) tools.

2. Information we collect

Nemo Factory collects nothing. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, location or contacts, and the events you create are never sent to us. We do not collect or share any identifying information, including advertising identifiers.

3. What is stored on your device, and iCloud sync

  • Events and categories — title, time, colour, done state, weekly repeat
  • Appearance settings — row size, alignment, start mark, grid style, palette, font, clock format, theme
  • Unlock purchase state — the result of the store receipt check (the fact of purchase only; no payment details)

All of it lives in the app's local database and is deleted with the app.

iCloud sync (iOS only, off by default). When you turn it on in Settings, your events and category names are stored in your own private iCloud database and kept in step with your other devices on the same Apple Account. Appearance settings and purchase state are not uploaded. The data sits in storage Apple provides — it never passes through Nemo Factory's servers, and we cannot see its contents. Turning it off stops further uploads, and you can delete what is already there in Settings › Apple Account › iCloud on your device. Android does not have this feature.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications — to show an event's start reminder on your device
  • Exact alarms — so reminders fire at the right minute
  • Widgets — to show today's plan on the home and lock screens

Every permission can be turned off in system settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.

5. Sharing and third parties

Because we collect nothing, we have nothing to share. We use no advertising and no analytics (tracking) tools.

6. Payments

The unlock is sold through App Store / Google Play in-app purchase. Nemo Factory never receives or stores card numbers or other payment details — only the fact that a purchase exists, stored on your device.

7. Storage and deletion

Everything is stored on your device and removed when you delete the app. We hold no user data, so there is nothing for us to erase.

8. Children's privacy

Griddy is not directed to children and collects no identifying information from anyone, children included. Guardians may contact us with any question about a child's use.

9. Your choices

Delete an event or delete the app to remove your data immediately. For any other question, email us at the address below.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will post changes on this page. Contact: contact@nemofactory.co.kr (Nemo Factory)
