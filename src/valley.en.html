Support

Tell us about anything — a problem, wrong information, a place we are missing. We reply as soon as we can. You can also write to us from Settings > Contact inside the app.

koreatong.kr@gmail.com

Q. The information about a place is wrong.

Place information is for reference and can differ from the site. Please check the local authority or the site office before visiting. Tell us through Settings > Contact or the email below and we will verify and correct it.

Q. I know a place that is not in the App.

Send the name and the location (address or a map link). We will check it and add it.

Q. I sent an enquiry and got no reply.

We cannot reply if you left the reply address blank. If you did fill it in, please check your spam folder, or email us directly at the address below.

Q. It says "Please try again later".

There is a daily limit on how many messages can be sent, to prevent spam. It resets after a day. For anything urgent, email us.

Q. What is free, and what needs a subscription?

Browsing, searching, and the map are always free. You can open two places in detail each day; after that you can watch a short ad to unlock three more for 24 hours. The AI trip planner is free once by watching a short ad. A subscription unlocks every place and unlimited trip plans with no ads: Monthly Pass is $4.99 per month and Yearly Pass is $29.99 per year; the exact amount in your currency is shown before you confirm.

Q. I paid, but the App still asks me to subscribe.

Open the subscription screen and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same store account you paid with. If it still fails, email us with the store you used and the date of purchase.

Q. How do I cancel?

On iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends, otherwise it renews. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.

Q. Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple and Google, not by us. On iPhone use reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android use the Play Store order history. We have no access to your payment and cannot issue a refund ourselves.

Q. Are there ads?

Only rewarded video ads, and only when you choose to watch one to unlock free content (the AI trip planner, or extra places). There are no banner or pop-up ads, and nothing plays unless you tap to watch it. These ads use your device advertising identifier (iOS IDFA / Android Advertising ID); on iOS we ask for tracking permission first and show a non-personalised ad if you decline. A subscription removes all ads. See section 13.

Q. How is my data stored?

Favourites, recently viewed places and settings are stored on your device only and disappear when you uninstall the App. The App does not collect your location. See sections 2 and 12 above.

Q. My favourites disappeared.

Favourites live on your device only. Uninstalling the App or changing phone loses them permanently — there is no account, so nothing is backed up on a server.

Q. What does the AI trip planner send?

Only the options you selected and the note you typed, plus a random identifier used to limit usage. See sections 2 and 7. Please do not type personal information into the note.

Q. Directions do not open.

Directions open in Kakao Map, Naver Map, Google Maps, or Apple Maps if installed; otherwise a web map opens. If it keeps failing, tell us which device you use.

Q. The weather is wrong.

Weather comes straight from the Korea Meteorological Administration short-term forecast. Forecasts differ from reality, and valleys change fast. Always trust the conditions you see on site.

  • After rain, valley water can rise suddenly. Rain upstream is dangerous downstream, even under clear sky.

  • Never enter closed areas or water without lifeguards.
  • Information in the App is for reference. Your safety is your own responsibility.

1. Introduction

Korea Water Escapes (the "App") is operated by an individual developer (the "Operator"). This policy explains what information the App handles, why, how long it is kept, and what you can do about it.

Effective date: August 3, 2026

2. Information we handle

The App works without an account. We do not collect your name, date of birth, gender, phone number, or postal address.

① Stored on your device only (never sent to the Operator): favourites, recently viewed places, map-app and display settings, launch count (used once to show a rating prompt), a random anti-spam identifier, and a cache of the place data. Deleting the App deletes all of it permanently; there is no account, so no server backup exists.

② Sent only when you tap Send on the Settings > Contact screen: the enquiry type, your message (up to 1,000 characters), an optional reply address, the random anti-spam identifier, the app version and platform (e.g. ios v1.0.2), and your IP address stored only as an irreversible hash for rate limiting. Nothing else from inside the App (favourites, browsing history) is attached.

③ Sent when you use the AI trip planner: the trip options you choose (number of days, regions, whether you have a rental car, whether you travel with children) and the free-text note you type, plus the random device identifier and an IP hash used to enforce usage limits. The note is truncated and stripped of control characters before use. Do not type personal or sensitive information into it.

④ Sent when you subscribe or when the App checks an existing subscription: the receipt token issued by the App Store or Google Play. The token proves that a subscription is active; it contains no name, card number, or billing address. We never receive your payment details.

⑤ Sent to Unity LevelPlay and its connected ad networks only when you choose to watch a rewarded ad: your device advertising identifier (iOS IDFA / Android Advertising ID) and the standard ad-request data those networks need to serve and measure the ad. On iOS this happens only after you allow tracking; if you decline, a non-personalised ad is shown. See section 13.

⑥ Not collected at all: real-time location (GPS), contacts, photos and media, microphone, call logs, calendar, and file access.

3. How information is collected

· Entered and sent by you through the Contact screen or the AI trip planner

· Generated on your device while using the App (settings, favourites, anti-spam identifier)

The following features contact external servers. By the nature of network requests, those servers may log your IP address and the time of the request. The App does not attach any other personal information to them.

· Maps — OpenStreetMap tile servers and a map library CDN: the coordinates of the map area shown

· Place data and notice updates — Supabase storage: a file request containing no personal information

· Enquiries and AI trip planner — Supabase Edge Functions: the items listed in section 2

4. Why we use it

· To provide the service and keep place information up to date

· To answer your enquiries and fix reported problems

· To generate a trip plan when you request one

· To prevent abuse (spam, automated requests) and to keep running costs bounded

We do not use the information for any other purpose. If the purpose changes, we will say so in this policy before the change takes effect.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or rent your information. Advertising in the English edition is served through Unity LevelPlay mediation (currently Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize). Unity and the connected ad networks receive the advertising identifier and ad-request data described in section 2 ⑤ to show and measure ads; see section 13.

We disclose information only when you have agreed in advance, or when required by law or by a lawful request from an investigative authority.

6. Processors

The following providers process data on the Operator’s behalf. Each follows its own privacy policy.

· Supabase Inc. — hosting of place data, storage and delivery of enquiries, and the server function that runs the AI trip planner

· OpenAI, L.L.C. — generates the trip plan text from the options and note you submit

· Unity Technologies (Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads) — mediates and serves the rewarded video ads in the English edition and measures ad performance, using the advertising identifier and ad-request data in section 2 ⑤

· Liftoff Mobile, Inc. (Liftoff Monetize) — serves rewarded video ads through Unity LevelPlay and measures ad performance, using the same items

· Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc.) — forwards received enquiries to the Operator’s mailbox

· Apple Inc. and Google LLC — take the payment, run the subscription, and answer the server’s question of whether a receipt token is valid. Billing data stays with them and is never passed to the Operator.

· OpenStreetMap Foundation — map tiles

If you tap directions, the App opens Kakao Map, Naver Map, Google Maps, or Apple Maps and passes only the fixed coordinates of the place. Anything after that is governed by that provider’s own policy. Changes to this list will be published in this policy.

7. International transfers

The App is operated from the Republic of Korea, and data you send is processed there and in the countries listed below.

· Supabase Inc. — the database region is set to Seoul, Republic of Korea, but the operator of the service is based outside Korea. Items: enquiry content, optional reply address, random identifier, app version, IP hash, and AI planner inputs. Transferred over an encrypted network connection when you send them. Retention: as stated in section 8.

· OpenAI, L.L.C. — United States. Items: the trip options and free-text note you submit through the AI trip planner. Transferred over an encrypted network connection at the moment you request a plan, for the sole purpose of generating the plan text. Retention follows OpenAI’s API policy for API traffic; the content is not used to train models.

· Unity Technologies (Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads) — United States and other countries where Unity operates data centres. Items: the advertising identifier and ad-request data in section 2 ⑤. Sent when you choose to watch a rewarded ad, to serve and measure that ad. Retention follows Unity’s policy.

· Liftoff Mobile, Inc. (Liftoff Monetize) — United States. Items: the same as above. Sent when Unity LevelPlay fills the ad with this network. Retention follows that company’s policy.

· Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc.) — United States. Items: enquiry content and optional reply address. Transferred when an enquiry is received, for the purpose of sending a notification email. Deleted according to that service’s policy after delivery.

· Apple Inc. and Google LLC — United States. Items: the subscription receipt token. Sent to that store’s verification server at the moment you subscribe, restore, or request an AI trip plan, for the sole purpose of confirming the subscription is active. The token is not stored by the Operator.

If you do not want these transfers, do not use the Contact screen, the AI trip planner, or the subscription. Every other part of the App works without them.

8. How long it is kept

· Data stored on your device: until you delete it or uninstall the App

· Enquiries received by the Operator: until the enquiry is resolved and the related problem is fixed, then deleted without delay. If you ask us to delete it earlier, we will do so once we can identify it.

· Anti-spam identifier and IP hash: until the anti-abuse purpose is met, then deleted

· AI planner inputs: not stored by the App beyond the request. Only a counter marker (no content) is kept to enforce usage limits.

· Subscription receipt token: not stored. It is checked against the store on each request and discarded. Only a marker counting how many devices share one subscription is kept, to limit account sharing.

· Where a law requires a longer retention period, that period applies.

9. Deletion

Information that has reached the end of its retention period or purpose is deleted without delay. Electronic files are erased in a way that cannot be recovered.

10. Your rights

· Data on your device: delete it in the App, or uninstall the App.

· Anything else: email koreatong.kr@gmail.com and we will act without delay.

There is no account system, so to identify your data we need the reply address you used or part of the message you sent.

12. Location

The App does not collect your real-time location and does not request location permission.

Maps are based on the fixed coordinates of the place you opened (a valley, beach, or hot spring). Those coordinates do not identify you.

13. Advertising

The English edition shows rewarded video ads served through Unity LevelPlay mediation (currently Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize). Ads appear only when you choose to watch one to unlock free content (an AI trip plan, or additional places); they are never forced and no banner or pop-up ads are shown.

To serve and measure these ads, those ad networks use your device advertising identifier (iOS IDFA / Android Advertising ID) and standard ad-request data. On iOS we ask for App Tracking permission first; if you decline, a non-personalised ad is shown instead.

A subscription removes all ads. You can reset or limit the advertising identifier in your device settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and Apple Advertising).

A subscription removes all ads. You can reset or limit the advertising identifier in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

On iOS, "tracking" means linking the advertising identifier with third-party data for advertising. If you deny the App Tracking Transparency prompt, the identifier is not used to track you and only non-personalised ads are shown. How Unity and its connected ad networks handle this data is governed by their own policies (Unity player and app user privacy policy, Unity privacy policy, Liftoff privacy policy).

Affiliate links. Screens such as the trip planner may show affiliate links for eSIM or car rental. Tapping one opens the partner’s own site, and from that point their privacy policy applies. Partners identify the referral themselves in order to settle commission. The Operator receives no personal data from them, and nothing is sent unless you tap the link.
